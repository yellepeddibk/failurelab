# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness and being respectful of differing viewpoints.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to project maintainers.
