# Engineering Onboarding Handbook

New engineers complete environment setup, a guided deploy, and a shadow on-call
shift within the first three weeks. The shadow shift carries no paging
responsibility.

Every new engineer is assigned an onboarding partner who is not their manager. The
partner answers questions, reviews the first several pull requests, and confirms
that the new engineer has working access to every system they need.

The first independent on-call shift happens no earlier than the sixth week and only
after the shadow shift is complete.
