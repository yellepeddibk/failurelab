# Expense Policy Handbook

Expenses under two hundred dollars require no pre-approval and are reimbursed after
submission with a receipt. Expenses above that threshold require manager approval
before the expense is incurred.

Overnight on-call shifts carry a fixed stipend paid monthly. The stipend is set by
the people team and is unrelated to the number of pages received during the shift.

Travel is booked through the corporate system. Reimbursement for travel booked
outside that system requires a written exception.
