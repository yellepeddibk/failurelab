# Remote Work Handbook

Engineering is remote-first. Meetings default to video with an agenda circulated in
advance, and every recurring meeting has a written summary so that attendance is
optional for anyone not directly involved.

Core collaboration hours are four hours per day overlapping the majority of the
team. Outside core hours, asynchronous communication is expected and no response is
required until the next working day.

Equipment is provided for a home workspace. Equipment requests go through the people
team and do not require manager approval below the standard threshold.
