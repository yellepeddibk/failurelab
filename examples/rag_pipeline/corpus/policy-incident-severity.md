# Incident Severity Policy

Severity one means a complete outage of a customer-facing service or confirmed data
loss. It pages the escalation owner, the engineering manager, and the incident
commander on duty immediately.

Severity two means significant degradation with a workaround available, or an
outage confined to a single region. It pages the escalation owner only.

Severity three means limited degradation with no customer-visible impact, or a
failure of an internal tool. It creates a ticket and does not page anyone outside
business hours.

Severity is set by the first responder and may be raised at any time. Lowering a
severity requires agreement from the incident commander.
