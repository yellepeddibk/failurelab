# On-Call Rotation Policy

Each service has a designated escalation owner for every shift. The escalation
owner is the first responder for any alert routed to that service and is expected
to acknowledge within five minutes during business hours and fifteen minutes
overnight.

Rotations run Monday to Monday. A secondary escalation owner covers any shift where
the primary is unavailable, and the handoff is recorded in the rotation calendar.

An escalation owner who cannot respond must transfer the shift explicitly. Silent
handoffs are not permitted, because an unacknowledged alert escalates to the
engineering manager after twenty minutes.

Compensation for overnight shifts is described in the employee handbook and is not
managed by the engineering organization.
