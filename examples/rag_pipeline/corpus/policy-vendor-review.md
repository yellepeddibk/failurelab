# Vendor Review Policy

Any vendor that processes customer data requires a security review before the
contract is signed. The review covers data handling, subprocessor disclosure,
breach notification terms, and deletion guarantees.

Reviews are repeated annually for vendors handling customer data and every two
years for all others. A vendor that fails a review is placed on a remediation plan
with a fixed deadline.

Engineering may not send customer data to an unreviewed vendor, including for
evaluation or proof-of-concept work.
