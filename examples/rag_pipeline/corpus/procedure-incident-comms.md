# Incident Communication Procedure

The incident commander owns all external communication. Engineers working the
incident do not post status updates directly to customers.

The first customer-facing update is published within thirty minutes of a severity
one or severity two declaration, even when the cause is unknown. Updates continue
every thirty minutes until the incident is resolved.

Internal updates go to the incident channel every fifteen minutes and include what
is known, what is being tried, and what is needed. Speculation is labeled as
speculation.

The final update states impact, duration, and whether a postmortem will follow.
