# Postmortem Authoring Procedure

A postmortem is required for every severity one and severity two incident, and for
any incident where a customer reported the problem before monitoring detected it.

The document states impact in customer terms, a timeline with detection and
recovery times, the contributing causes, and action items with named owners and
dates. Postmortems are blameless. Naming an individual as a cause is not permitted.

The draft is circulated within five business days and reviewed within ten. Action
items are tracked in the normal engineering backlog and are not allowed to expire
silently.
